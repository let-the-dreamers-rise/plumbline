The tape is a public H100 index of what renters proved they paid per GPU-hour. Each entry is a zkTLS proof of an invoice or a price page, checked onchain: attestor signature, registered source, freshness, and a receipt so one invoice counts once. With proofs from three or more providers the program publishes a weighted median, shown below next to the posted list price.

The program runs end to end on a local Solana validator today; its devnet deploy is pending and no price proofs have been published yet. The tape is designed to feed a Hyperliquid HIP-3 perp; that relayer is dry-run tested and a testnet market is pending.
