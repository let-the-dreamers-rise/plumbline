Prove the fleet. Keep the numbers.

Each week you produce one proof that a GPU answering your lender's fresh challenge is genuine NVIDIA hardware, and that your revenue covers debt service. Each proof covers one GPU and does not show who owns it; proving every GPU in the fleet is planned. The proof goes onchain; your individual payouts, tenants and prices never do (the period's total revenue and coverage are public). Planned pricing: a facility fee plus 5 to 15 basis points a year on the loan, weekly proofs included, paid by the lender or passed to the borrower in the loan terms. Today the program charges one flat fee on every proof (0.0013 SOL of test SOL in the devnet demo). Verifying is free.

What you need

Hardware

NVIDIA H100 with confidential computing enabled (H200 untested), inside an Intel TDX confidential VM (for example Phala Cloud dstack, or any Intel TDX host with the NVIDIA driver in CC mode; not yet run on one).

A payout record

A Razorpay, Stripe or marketplace payout account that pays you for GPU time. The prover reads it over zkTLS; only amount, date and a payout id are extracted. Individual payouts stay on your machine; the proof publishes only the window's total revenue, the coverage ratio and the number of claims.

Loan terms

Your lender opens the loan on the program with debt service, period and the minimum coverage (11,500 basis points is 1.15x). No lender yet? Request a proof below and we open a self-declared term sheet for the first proofs.

The four steps

Get the challenge

Your lender runs issue_gpu_challenge; a 32-byte nonce lands in the loan's covenant account. Read it with npm run loan:show. The GPU must sign exactly this nonce, so last week's report cannot be reused.

Collect evidence on the machine

Ask the GPU for its attestation report with the challenge as the SPDM nonce, ask the enclave for a TDX quote whose report_data commits to that report and the challenge, and run npm run prove:revenue for the period's payouts. Exact commands and formats are in the docs and zk/README.md.

Run the prover

cd zk/risc0 && cargo run --release -- groth16 verifies all three roots inside the guest, tests coverage, and writes a 256-byte Groth16 seal plus the public journal. Needs Docker and about 16 GB of RAM; runs on your own machine so nothing leaves it.

Submit

Send the seal and journal to prove_covenant (CLI in the docs). The program checks the proof, the challenge, the guest image and the attestor set, then records PASS or WATCH. Your public page is /loan/<address> and your badge is /badge/<address>.svg.

Today the prover runs on the bundled sample evidence (NVIDIA's published H100 report, a published H200 TDX quote and test-signed revenue claims). A mode that takes your own report, quote and claims is planned; the steps above describe the target flow.
