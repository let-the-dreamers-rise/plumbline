What runs before every draw

Six checks. One transaction. Then the money moves.

  1. 01

    Lender issues a challenge

    A fresh nonce for this period, so an old answer cannot be reused.

    issue_gpu_challenge · signed by the lender
  2. 02

    The GPU answers inside the enclave

    The H100 signs an SPDM report over the challenge with its own P-384 key, inside a confidential VM.

    spdm report · chain to NVIDIA Device Identity CA
  3. 03

    Attested to Intel's root

    The Intel TDX quote names the VM image the lender pinned, and binds the GPU to it.

    tdx quote v4 · Intel SGX Root CA 44a0196b…74d3
  4. 04

    Revenue proven, kept private

    zkTLS proofs of the period's payouts. The total is public; each payout stays private.

    reclaim zkTLS · pinned attestor set
  5. 05

    One zero-knowledge proof

    A RISC Zero guest checks all of it against the 1.15x floor and compresses about 32 KB of evidence into 256 bytes.

    risc0 → groth16 · 256 B · ~100 min to prove
  6. 06

    Solana decides the draw

    The program verifies the proof, uses up the challenge, then opens the draw or refuses it.

    prove_covenant · ~120k CU · draw_private

Straight answers.

Is this live?

The lending oracle is live on Solana devnet: a 1.15x proof was verified and its draw approved, a 1.14x draw was refused, and a replay was refused. Mainnet is refused everywhere until the verifier is audited.

What does one proof show, and what not?

That one attested GPU answered the lender's fresh challenge, ran in a known enclave, and its proven payouts cover the floor. It does not show who owns the GPU; weekly fleet sampling is planned.

What is real in the demo today?

The proofs verify NVIDIA's public sample H100 report and a published H200 TDX quote, chain-verified to their roots. Revenue claims are synthetic, signed by two test attestor keys. No TCB or revocation checks yet.

Who reads the result?

The lender's program gates the draw. The verify page, badge and JSON are free for curators, depositors and GPU networks.
